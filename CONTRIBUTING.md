# Contributing to the research proposal

Help make the question testable. The design is open to revision; the purpose is not to defend a preferred architecture.

## Issues

Include the question or design choice, your suggestion or counterexample, why it matters, and what evidence would support or undermine it. Cite sources where applicable and distinguish reported findings from interpretation.

Useful topics include trajectory-level replication, seed disclosure, neutral re-entry cues, task-generation rules, information-matched controls, artifact retirement, resource accounting and boundary behavior. You need not solve the entire protocol to contribute.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused and explain their effect on the research question. Prefer DESIGN-NOTES.md for unresolved suggestions. PROPOSAL.md and FIELD-NOTE.md preserve the initial published texts; propose substantive revisions separately with a clear version history rather than silently rewriting those snapshots.

Do not add private transcripts, credentials, account data, production inputs or material you are not entitled to share. This repository is for the disclosed research proposal, not unrelated internal implementations.

## Evidence and decisions

Label hypothetical examples, proposed tests and actual results distinctly. A suggestion or merged documentation change does not authorize execution or freeze a protocol. Experimental reports should identify the model and environment, exact tested version, resources, operator interventions, unsuccessful attempts and limitations. Do not post private model reasoning traces; observable actions, outputs and appropriate usage records are sufficient.

Host-blocked actions, participant attempts, task errors and infrastructure faults are distinct observations. Preserve null results, non-use and sensible decisions not to build. For eventual protocol versions, retain exact frozen documents and record deviations separately; post hoc changes must not be presented as predeclared choices.

## Discussion venues

Use GitHub Issues and pull requests for actionable design work. Broader discussion is welcome on [the HF proposal thread](https://discuss.huggingface.co/t/research-proposal-what-does-an-agent-build-to-sustain-its-own-work/191361). Link related discussions so objections and resolutions remain traceable.
